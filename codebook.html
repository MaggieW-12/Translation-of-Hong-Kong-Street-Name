Scope and method範圍與方法

The unit is an official bilingual street or road name recorded by the Hong Kong Transport Department. The analytic direction is Traditional Chinese to English, but an official pair does not prove which name came first. These categories describe the observable relationship between the two printed forms, not a reconstructed historical intention.

編碼單位是香港運輸署記錄的官方雙語街道或道路名稱。分析方向是由繁體中文到英文， 但官方名稱對不能證明哪個名稱先出現。以下類別描述兩個刊印形式之間的可觀察關係， 而不是重構當時的命名意圖。

The method follows the broad distinction in Vinay and Darbelnet (1958) between borrowing and calque, with supporting ideas from Newmark's (1988) account of transference and literal translation. Evidence comes from two reproducible signals: Cantonese Jyutping similarity and a fixed English-to-Chinese semantic glossary.

方法主要採用維內與達貝爾內（Vinay & Darbelnet, 1958）對借用與仿譯的區分， 並參考紐馬克（Newmark, 1988）關於轉寫及直譯的討論。判斷證據來自兩項可重現訊號： 粵語粵拼相似度，以及固定的英中語義詞表。

1. Phonetic transliteration1. 音譯

The English name represents the sound of the Cantonese name, usually syllable by syllable, rather than translating its individual meaning.

英文名稱主要再現粵語名稱的聲音，通常逐音節轉寫，而不是翻譯個別字義。

Theory source
Vinay & Darbelnet (1958), borrowing; Newmark (1988), transcription.
Decision rule
Cleaned English has at least 0.50 character similarity with the Jyutping of the Chinese name, and no semantic glossary item matches. 清理後的英文與中文名稱的粵語粵拼有至少 0.50 的字元相似度，而且未命中語義詞表。
Positive example
汀逸路 → TING YAT ROAD (score 1.000)
Negative example
油街 → OIL STREET. The meaning matches, but the sound does not.
Boundary case
A name is not phonetic merely because its English spelling looks traditional. Printed sound similarity must be measurable. 英文拼寫看起來傳統，並不自動構成音譯；刊印形式的語音相似度必須可以量度。

2. Semantic translation2. 意譯

At least one English content word translates a meaning present in the Chinese name, while the whole pair does not show a strong phonetic match.

至少一個英文實詞翻譯了中文名稱中存在的意義，而整個名稱對沒有強烈語音對應。

Theory source
Vinay & Darbelnet (1958), literal translation/calque; Newmark (1988), literal translation.
Decision rule
A fixed gloss such as OIL → 油 or MUSEUM → 博物館 appears in the pair, and the full-name phonetic score is below 0.50. 名稱對出現固定對應，如 OIL → 油 或 MUSEUM → 博物館，而且全名語音分數低於 0.50。
Positive example
油街 → OIL STREET; 博物館道 → MUSEUM DRIVE
Negative example
洪順路 → HUNG SHUN ROAD. HUNG and SHUN reproduce sound rather than translate meanings.
Boundary case
油街 → OIL STREET is semantic, not hybrid: the generic 街/STREET relation appears in every road name and does not establish a distinctive sound match. 油街 → OIL STREET 屬意譯而非混合：通用 街/STREET 關係幾乎見於所有道路名稱，不能構成獨特的語音對應。

3. Hybrid phonetic-semantic rendering3. 音意混合

One part of the name is represented by sound and another part is translated by meaning, producing a bilingual structure within the same name.

名稱的一部分以聲音再現，另一部分以意義翻譯，在同一名稱內形成雙語結構。

Theory source
Vinay & Darbelnet (1958), combined procedures; Newmark (1988), transference plus semantic translation.
Decision rule
The full name has a phonetic score of at least 0.50 and at least one semantic glossary item also matches. 全名語音分數至少 0.50，而且同時命中至少一個語義詞表項目。
Positive example
天后廟道 → TIN HAU TEMPLE ROAD. `TIN HAU` follows Cantonese sound; `TEMPLE` translates 廟.
Negative example
教堂街 → CHURCH STREET. The pair is overwhelmingly semantic and has no strong sound match.
Boundary case
Only 11 of 500 names are hybrids. A single soundlike syllable is not enough unless the full pair passes the threshold. 500 個名稱中只有 11 個屬混合類別；單一音近音節不足以構成混合，全名必須通過門檻。

4. Conventional or indirect rendering4. 慣用或間接譯名

The pair has neither the rule-based Cantonese sound match nor the semantic match recorded by the glossary. It may involve renaming, an established foreign proper name, abbreviation, or an opaque official convention.

名稱對既未通過規則化的粵語語音對應，也未命中語義詞表。它可能涉及改名、 既有外語專名、縮寫，或今人不易直接看出的官方慣例。

Theory source
Venuti (1995), historical and institutional norms; Newmark (1988), accepted translation and proper-name convention.
Decision rule
Both automated criteria fail. Assign provisionally and prioritize the row for human review. 兩項自動準則均未通過。先作暫定分類，並優先安排人工審核。
Positive example
碧翠路 → RAZOR HILL ROAD. Neither full sound nor the available meaning links is transparent.
Negative example
山頂道 → PEAK ROAD. PEAK → 山頂 is captured by the semantic rule.
Boundary case
南丁格爾路 → NIGHTINGALE ROAD is a sound-based treatment of an English proper name, but falls below the Cantonese-match rule. It stays here provisionally. 南丁格爾路 → NIGHTINGALE ROAD 是英語專名的音譯式處理，但未通過粵語對應準則，故暫列此類。
