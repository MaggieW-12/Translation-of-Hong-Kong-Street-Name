From street signs to systems 從街道招牌到名稱系統

How a bilingual name registry became a small tool for asking how Hong Kong streets travel between Chinese and English. 一個雙語名稱資料庫，如何成為探索香港街道名稱在中英之間轉換方式的工具。

Contents Origin Background Gap Questions Method Analysis Conclusion Reflection References

Origin緣起

I chose this topic because Hong Kong street names reveal language contact and history. They reflect layers of Cantonese usage, English influence, localization, and changing naming conventions in Hong Kong. Besides, they provide a large, officially documented set of short Chinese–English pairs in which different translation strategies are visible in everyday public space. The topic makes it possible to connect translation theory with a reproducible dataset, verify individual examples against an official source, and use interactive visualisations to compare patterns across hundreds of names.

我選擇研究香港街道名稱，因為這些名稱展現語言接觸與歷史。香港街道名稱反映粵語使用、英語影響、本地化，以及香港命名慣例的變化。此外，這些名稱構成一套由官方記錄、篇幅簡短的中英名稱對，讓不同的翻譯策略在公共空間中清晰可見。這項研究讓我能把翻譯理論連結到可重現的資料集，依據官方來源核對個別例子，並透過互動視覺化比較數百個名稱中的模式。

Background背景

Hong Kong's street names reflect several layers of naming practice. Some English names reconstruct Cantonese pronunciation; others translate meanings such as garden, hill, temple, or market; still others preserve British administrators, foreign places, abbreviations, and later renamings. An officially bilingual list makes these pairings easy to collect, but it does not tell the reader which translation strategy is operating.

香港街道名稱包含多重命名實踐。有些英文名稱重構粵語讀音；有些意譯花園、山、 廟或市場等意思；另一些則保留英國官員、外國地名、縮寫或後來改名的痕跡。 官方雙語列表方便收集中英文名稱，卻不會直接告訴讀者背後使用了哪種翻譯策略。

Gap研究缺口

Existing road datasets are designed for navigation and asset management. General translation theory gives useful categories such as borrowing, calque, and literal translation, but applying them to hundreds of official names requires a consistent rule, source traceability, and a view that lets a reader compare individual entries with the overall pattern.

現有道路資料主要服務導航及設施管理。 一般翻譯理論提供借用、仿譯、直譯等類別，但要把這些類別應用到數百個官方名稱，仍需要一致的規則、可追溯的來源，以及能讓讀者同時比較個別例子與整體模式的介面。

Questions研究問題

  • What proportion of the sample is primarily sound-based, meaning-based, mixed, or conventional?
  • Does the Cantonese-English sound score divide the categories as the codebook predicts?
  • How do these strategies combine with road types such as Road, Street, and Lane?
  • 樣本中主要依聲音、意義、混合方式或慣例形成的名稱各佔多少？
  • 粵英語音分數是否如編碼手冊預期般區分各類別？
  • 這些策略如何與 Road、Street、Lane 等道路類型結合？

Method方法

Source and sampling來源與抽樣

The source is the Transport Department's Road Network (2nd Generation), Road centerline file on DATA.GOV.HK, retrieved on 11 November 2026. I streamed the 460 MB GML file and kept only official English and Chinese name fields plus object and street identifiers. The parser removed geometry, placeholders, empty or script-mismatched names, long values, and non-road features. It then deduplicated bilingual pairs and normalized dash variants.

資料來源是 DATA.GOV.HK 上運輸署「第二代道路網絡」的道路中心線檔案，擷取日期為 2026 年 11 月 11 日。我串流讀取約 460 MB 的 GML 檔案，只保留官方中英文名稱、 對象編號及街道編號。處理程式移除幾何資料、佔位符、空白或文字系統不符的名稱、 過長項目及非道路要素，然後去除重複名稱對，並統一破折號格式。

3,319 after normalization collapsed 28 punctuation variants 統一格式後合併了 28 個標點變體

The first filters recorded 7,114 empty or placeholder values and 101 non-road features before deduplication. The final sample was not selected by preference: every eligible pair was sorted by SHA-256("3620:" + Chinese + TAB + English), and the lowest 500 hashes were retained.

初步篩選共記下 7,114 個空白或佔位符及 101 個非道路要素，重複資料其後才合併。 最終樣本並非按喜好選擇：所有合資格名稱對均按 SHA-256("3620:" + 中文 + TAB + 英文) 排序，只保留最低的 500 個雜湊值。

Coding and validation編碼與驗證

Four categories were defined before the final sample: phonetic transliteration, semantic translation, hybrid phonetic-semantic rendering, and conventional or indirect rendering. Cantonese Jyutping and a fixed semantic glossary supplied evidence; the rules are published in the codebook. I tested the rules on 30 examples and lowered the phonetic threshold from 0.52 to 0.50 because several transparent sound matches landed exactly at 0.50.

最終樣本產生前，我先定義四個類別：音譯、意譯、音意混合，以及慣用或間接譯名。 粵語粵拼及固定語義詞表提供分類證據，完整規則刊於編碼手冊。我先用 30 個例子測試， 並把語音門檻由 0.52 調低至 0.50，因為若干明顯音譯剛好落在 0.50。

Automated validation currently passes: 500 rows, 500 unique IDs, 500 unique pairs, and all 500 pairs found in the reduced official source cache. In addition, I have checked the deterministic 50-entry review sample against the official source. Those 50 rows are marked human_checked=yes; the remaining 450 remain no.

目前自動驗證通過：500 列、500 個唯一編號、500 對唯一名稱，而且全部 500 對 均可在縮減後的官方來源快取中找到。此外，我已對照官方來源核對 可重現的 50 項樣本。這 50 列已標記為 human_checked=yes， 其餘 450 列仍為 no。

Analysis分析

The corpus is strongly sound-oriented: 378 of 500 sampled names (75.6%) are phonetic transliterations. Semantic translation accounts for 55 names (11.0%), conventional or indirect rendering for 56 (11.2%), and hybrid rendering for 11 (2.2%).

語料明顯以聲音為主：500 個抽樣名稱中有 378 個（75.6%）屬音譯；意譯有 55 個 （11.0%）、慣用或間接譯名有 56 個（11.2%），音意混合只有 11 個（2.2%）。

Term network詞語網絡

The Term network asks which English name elements repeatedly connect to the same Chinese characters. Frequent elements such as TAI, TIN, WAN, CHUNG, and SHING create recurring phonetic clusters, while meaning-bearing links involving 山, 海, 村, or 安 expose a second layer. Clicking any node searches that exact element across both sides of the corpus.

「詞語網絡」詢問哪些英文名稱成分會反覆連繫到相同中文字。TAI、TIN、WAN、 CHUNG、SHING 等常見成分形成重複的音譯群組；而涉及山、海、村或安的連繫， 則顯示另一層以意義為基礎的關係。點擊任何節點，即會在中英雙語語料中搜尋該成分。

Strategy treemap策略矩形樹圖

The Strategy treemap asks how the four categories divide into road types. Road dominates the corpus, so its tiles are distributed across every strategy. Street and Lane also carry many phonetic names, reflecting how generic road words are paired with localized Cantonese elements. Clicking a tile limits both the heatmap and data grid to that category and road type.

「策略矩形樹圖」詢問四個類別如何分布到不同道路類型。Road 在語料中佔最多， 因此其色塊分布於所有策略；Street 與 Lane 亦包含大量音譯名稱，顯示通用道路詞 如何與本地化粵語成分結合。點擊任一色塊，會把熱圖及資料表同時限定於該類別與道路類型。

Sound-score heatmap語音分數熱圖

The Sound-score heatmap asks whether the categories behave differently on Cantonese-English similarity. The separation is clear but not absolute: all 378 phonetic names fall at or above 0.50, while semantic names fall entirely below it (32 in 0-.24 and 23 in .25-.49). Most conventional names also sit below 0.50, while hybrids cluster at 0.50 and above. Clicking a cell combines its category and score band as filters.

「語音分數熱圖」詢問各類別在粵英相似度上是否有不同表現。分隔清楚但並非絕對： 378 個音譯全部位於 0.50 或以上；意譯全部低於 0.50，其中 32 個在 0-.24， 23 個在 .25-.49。大部分慣用譯名亦低於 0.50，而混合類別集中於 0.50 以上。 點擊方格會把其類別及分數區間合併為篩選條件。

Data grid資料表

The data grid provides the audit layer. Sorting, searching, and exporting use exactly the same filtered row set as all three charts. This makes it possible to check whether a visible pattern is supported by the underlying entries rather than only by a chart's geometry.

「資料表」提供核查層。排序、搜尋及匯出均使用與三幅圖完全相同的篩選資料， 因此可以檢查圖中的模式是否真正由底層名稱支持，而不只是圖形產生的印象。

Conclusion結論

The analysis shows that Hong Kong's bilingual street names are not one translation method repeated 500 times. Phonetic transliteration is the dominant strategy, but semantic and conventional names form a substantial minority. The combination of network, hierarchy, distribution, and table makes those strategies legible at both the individual and aggregate level.

分析顯示，香港雙語街道名稱不是同一種翻譯方法重複五百次。音譯是最主要的策略， 但意譯及慣用名稱也構成不可忽略的少數。網絡、層級、分布與表格的組合， 讓這些策略既可在單一名稱層面閱讀，也可在整體層面比較。

Reflection反思

What I learned

Working with 500 official Hong Kong street-name pairs taught me that translation is not simply a matter of checking whether words correspond. The English and Chinese forms can preserve sound, communicate meaning, combine both methods, or rely on a conventional and historically established name. The most useful question is therefore not “Is this translation correct?” but “What observable relationship exists between the two names?”

透過研究 500 對香港官方街道名稱，我學到翻譯不只是檢查詞語是否對應。中英文名稱可以保留原有讀法、傳達意義、結合兩種方法，或沿用一個慣用而具歷史基礎的名稱。因此，最有用的問題並不是「這個翻譯是否正確？」，而是「兩個名稱之間存在甚麼可觀察的關係？」

What was most difficult

The hardest problem was defining categories that remain consistent across names with different historical backgrounds. An official bilingual pair proves that two forms coexist, but it does not prove which form came first or what the original naming intention was. I therefore had to describe observable relationships rather than make unsupported claims about translation history.

最困難的地方，是在歷史背景不同的名稱之間建立一致的分類。一對官方雙語名稱證明兩種形式同時存在，卻不證明哪一種形式先出現，也不證明原本的命名意圖。因此，我描述能夠觀察的關係，而不對翻譯歷史作沒有根據的推斷。

What I would do differently

If I repeated the project, I would validate a larger random sample throughout the project rather than concentrating human checking near the end. I would also test the website with users to confirm that they understand what every chart represents. The network, treemap, and heatmap all needed clearer explanations than their first versions provided.

如果重新進行這個項目，我會在整個項目期間驗證較大的隨機樣本，而不是把人工核對集中在最後階段。我亦會請使用者測試網站，確認他們理解每幅圖表所代表的內容。最初的網絡圖、矩形樹圖及熱圖，都需要比第一版更清楚的說明。

What AI could do使用 AI 的好處

AI was useful for locating and processing the official dataset, cleaning and deduplicating names, creating a reproducible sample, applying rules consistently, and building the linked website. It could also check that row counts and chart totals matched, detect broken layouts, and help draft explanations of the method.

AI 有助尋找及處理官方資料集、清理及去除重複名稱、建立可重現樣本、一致套用規則，以及建立互相連結的網站。其也能核對行數與圖表總數是否一致、偵測版面錯誤，並協助草擬方法說明。

What AI could not do使用 AI 的弊端

AI could not decide on my behalf what a translation means or what its historical origin was. It could not reliably resolve ambiguous names, complete an inadequate semantic glossary, or take responsibility for the final interpretation. It also made a serious contextual error when it initially placed the old humor corpus in the new street-name project; I had to notice that the file did not match the topic.

AI 不能替我決定一個翻譯的意思或其歷史來源。AI 無法可靠地解決含糊名稱、補足不完整的語義詞表，也無法為最終詮釋負責。另外，其還犯了一個重要的語境錯誤：最初把不相關的幽默語料放入新的街道名稱項目；我必須自己發現該檔案與題目不符。

AI therefore accelerated the work, but it did not replace human judgement. My role was to choose the topic, define what counted as evidence, inspect official examples, question unclear categories, and remain responsible for the final claims. AI could help me produce and test a system, but I still had to decide what that system’s results meant.

因此，AI 加快了工作，卻沒有取代人的判斷。我的角色是選擇研究甚麼主題、界定甚麼算作證據、檢查官方例子、質疑不清楚的分類，並為最終主張負責。AI 可以協助我建立及測試系統，但系統結果的意義仍必須由我判斷。
