From street signs to systems 從街道招牌到名稱系統

How a bilingual name registry became a small tool for asking how Hong Kong streets travel between Chinese and English. 一個雙語名稱資料庫，如何成為探索香港街道名稱在中英之間轉換方式的工具。

Contents Origin Background Gap Questions Method Analysis Conclusion Reflection References

Origin緣起

Hong Kong street signs make translation visible in everyday life. A single bilingual sign may preserve a Cantonese sound, translate a landscape word, mix both strategies, or retain a name whose connection to the present is historical rather than transparent.

香港的街道牌讓翻譯成為日常生活的一部分。同一塊中英雙語路牌，可能保留粵語聲音、 意譯地貌詞、混用兩種方式，或沿用一個今天已不那麼透明的歷史名稱。

I chose this topic because the names are public, short, and locally meaningful. They also invite a question that is larger than individual translation choices: what kinds of linguistic work does a city perform when it keeps two naming systems visible at once?

我選擇這個題目，因為街道名稱公開、簡短，而且與本地生活緊密相連。它們更帶出一個 超過單一翻譯選擇的問題：當一座城市同時展示兩套命名系統時，它實際上進行了怎樣的語言工作？

Background背景

Hong Kong's street names reflect several layers of naming practice. Some English names reconstruct Cantonese pronunciation; others translate meanings such as garden, hill, temple, or market; still others preserve British administrators, foreign places, abbreviations, and later renamings. An officially bilingual list makes these pairings easy to collect, but it does not tell the reader which strategy is operating.

香港街道名稱包含多重命名實踐。有些英文名稱重構粵語讀音；有些意譯花園、山、 廟或市場等意思；另一些則保留英國官員、外國地名、縮寫或後來改名的痕跡。 官方雙語列表方便收集名稱對，卻不會直接告訴讀者背後使用了哪種策略。

Gap研究缺口

Existing road datasets are designed for navigation and asset management. They answer where a road is, not how its two names relate. General translation theory gives useful categories such as borrowing, calque, and literal translation, but applying them to hundreds of official names requires a consistent rule, source traceability, and a view that lets a reader compare individual entries with the overall pattern.

現有道路資料主要服務導航及設施管理。它們回答道路在哪裡，而不是兩個名稱如何相關。 一般翻譯理論提供借用、仿譯、直譯等類別，但要把這些類別應用到數百個官方名稱， 仍需要一致的規則、可追溯的來源，以及能讓讀者同時比較個別例子與整體模式的介面。

Questions研究問題

  • What proportion of the sample is primarily sound-based, meaning-based, mixed, or conventional?
  • Does the Cantonese-English sound score divide the categories as the codebook predicts?
  • How do these strategies combine with road types such as Road, Street, and Lane?
  • What can recurring English name elements reveal that a single example cannot?
  • 樣本中主要依聲音、意義、混合方式或慣例形成的名稱各佔多少？
  • 粵英語音分數是否如編碼手冊預期般區分各類別？
  • 這些策略如何與 Road、Street、Lane 等道路類型結合？
  • 反覆出現的英文名稱成分能揭示哪些單一例子看不到的現象？

Method方法

Source and sampling來源與抽樣

The source is the Transport Department's Road Network (2nd Generation), Road centerline file on DATA.GOV.HK, retrieved on 11 November 2026. I streamed the 460 MB GML file and kept only official English and Chinese name fields plus object and street identifiers. The parser removed geometry, placeholders, empty or script-mismatched names, long values, and non-road features. It then deduplicated bilingual pairs and normalized dash variants.

資料來源是 DATA.GOV.HK 上運輸署「第二代道路網絡」的道路中心線檔案，擷取日期為 2026 年 11 月 11 日。我串流讀取約 460 MB 的 GML 檔案，只保留官方中英文名稱、 對象編號及街道編號。處理程式移除幾何資料、佔位符、空白或文字系統不符的名稱、 過長項目及非道路要素，然後去除重複名稱對，並統一破折號格式。

3,347 raw unique pairs after the first filters 初步篩選後的原始唯一名稱對
3,319 after normalization collapsed 28 punctuation variants 統一格式後合併了 28 個標點變體
500 lowest SHA-256 ranks with seed 3620 以種子 3620 選取最低 SHA-256 排名
2,819 eligible pairs not selected by the seed 未獲種子選中的合資格名稱對

The first filters recorded 7,114 empty or placeholder values and 101 non-road features before deduplication. The final sample was not selected by preference: every eligible pair was sorted by SHA-256("3620:" + Chinese + TAB + English), and the lowest 500 hashes were retained.

初步篩選共記下 7,114 個空白或佔位符及 101 個非道路要素，重複資料其後才合併。 最終樣本並非按喜好選擇：所有合資格名稱對均按 SHA-256("3620:" + 中文 + TAB + 英文) 排序，只保留最低的 500 個雜湊值。

Coding and validation編碼與驗證

Four categories were defined before the final sample: phonetic transliteration, semantic translation, hybrid phonetic-semantic rendering, and conventional or indirect rendering. Cantonese Jyutping and a fixed semantic glossary supplied evidence; the rules are published in the codebook. I tested the rules on 30 examples and lowered the phonetic threshold from 0.52 to 0.50 because several transparent sound matches landed exactly at 0.50.

最終樣本產生前，我先定義四個類別：音譯、意譯、音意混合，以及慣用或間接譯名。 粵語粵拼及固定語義詞表提供分類證據，完整規則刊於編碼手冊。我先用 30 個例子測試， 並把語音門檻由 0.52 調低至 0.50，因為若干明顯音譯剛好落在 0.50。

Automated validation currently passes: 500 rows, 500 unique IDs, 500 unique pairs, and all 500 pairs found in the reduced official source cache. This is a source-match check, not the brief's required personal check. A deterministic 50-entry review queue has been prepared, but no row is yet marked human_checked=yes.

目前自動驗證通過：500 列、500 個唯一編號、500 對唯一名稱，而且全部 500 對 均可在縮減後的官方來源快取中找到。這是來源比對，不等同作業要求的個人核對。 我已準備一份可重現的 50 項核對清單，但目前尚未有任何一列標記為 human_checked=yes。

Analysis分析

The corpus is strongly sound-oriented: 378 of 500 sampled names (75.6%) are phonetic transliterations. Semantic translation accounts for 55 names (11.0%), conventional or indirect rendering for 56 (11.2%), and hybrid rendering for 11 (2.2%).

語料明顯以聲音為主：500 個抽樣名稱中有 378 個（75.6%）屬音譯；意譯有 55 個 （11.0%）、慣用或間接譯名有 56 個（11.2%），音意混合只有 11 個（2.2%）。

Term network詞語網絡

The Term network asks which English name elements repeatedly connect to the same Chinese characters. Frequent elements such as TAI, TIN, WAN, CHUNG, and SHING create recurring phonetic clusters, while meaning-bearing links involving 山, 海, 村, or 安 expose a second layer. Clicking any node searches that exact element across both sides of the corpus.

「詞語網絡」詢問哪些英文名稱成分會反覆連繫到相同中文字。TAI、TIN、WAN、 CHUNG、SHING 等常見成分形成重複的音譯群組；而涉及山、海、村或安的連繫， 則顯示另一層以意義為基礎的關係。點擊任何節點，即會在中英雙語語料中搜尋該成分。

Strategy treemap策略矩形樹圖

The Strategy treemap asks how the four categories divide into road types. Road dominates the corpus, so its tiles are distributed across every strategy. Street and Lane also carry many phonetic names, reflecting how generic road words are paired with localized Cantonese elements. Clicking a tile limits both the heatmap and data grid to that category and road type.

「策略矩形樹圖」詢問四個類別如何分布到不同道路類型。Road 在語料中佔最多， 因此其色塊分布於所有策略；Street 與 Lane 亦包含大量音譯名稱，顯示通用道路詞 如何與本地化粵語成分結合。點擊任一色塊，會把熱圖及資料表同時限定於該類別與道路類型。

Sound-score heatmap語音分數熱圖

The Sound-score heatmap asks whether the categories behave differently on Cantonese-English similarity. The separation is clear but not absolute: all 378 phonetic names fall at or above 0.50, while semantic names fall entirely below it (32 in 0-.24 and 23 in .25-.49). Most conventional names also sit below 0.50, while hybrids cluster at 0.50 and above. Clicking a cell combines its category and score band as filters.

「語音分數熱圖」詢問各類別在粵英相似度上是否有不同表現。分隔清楚但並非絕對： 378 個音譯全部位於 0.50 或以上；意譯全部低於 0.50，其中 32 個在 0-.24， 23 個在 .25-.49。大部分慣用譯名亦低於 0.50，而混合類別集中於 0.50 以上。 點擊方格會把其類別及分數區間合併為篩選條件。

Data grid資料表

The data grid provides the audit layer. Sorting, searching, and exporting use exactly the same filtered row set as all three charts. This makes it possible to check whether a visible pattern is supported by the underlying entries rather than only by a chart's geometry.

「資料表」提供核查層。排序、搜尋及匯出均使用與三幅圖完全相同的篩選資料， 因此可以檢查圖中的模式是否真正由底層名稱支持，而不只是圖形產生的印象。

Conclusion結論

The sample shows that Hong Kong's bilingual street names are not one translation method repeated 500 times. Sound is the dominant strategy, but semantic and conventional names form a substantial minority. The combination of network, hierarchy, distribution, and table makes those strategies legible at both the individual and aggregate level.

樣本顯示，香港雙語街道名稱不是同一種翻譯方法重複五百次。聲音是最主要的策略， 但意譯及慣用名稱也構成不可忽略的少數。網絡、層級、分布與表格的組合， 讓這些策略既可在單一名稱層面閱讀，也可在整體層面比較。

Reflection反思

The most difficult problem was not collecting data but defining a category that is consistent across names with different histories. An official pair proves that two forms coexist; it does not prove which was translated first. I therefore describe observable relationships rather than reconstructing intentions. The semantic glossary is also incomplete, so “conventional or indirect” remains a review category, not a final claim.

最困難的地方不是收集資料，而是為歷史背景不同的名稱建立一致分類。官方名稱對證明 兩種形式同時存在，但不證明哪一個先被翻譯。因此，我描述可觀察的關係，而不推測命名者 的意圖。語義詞表並不完整，所以「慣用或間接」仍是待審核類別，而非最終定論。

I would improve the project by personally checking the 50 queued entries, adding a second annotation pass after a break, and reporting agreement. I would also test whether historical street-name sources can distinguish Chinese-origin names from English-origin names more reliably.

下一步，我會親自核對 50 個已排定的項目，在相隔一段時間後再編碼一次，並報告一致性。 我也會嘗試使用歷史街道命名資料，更可靠地區分中文起源與英文起源的名稱。
